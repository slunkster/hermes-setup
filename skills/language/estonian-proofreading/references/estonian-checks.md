# Estonian checks — annotated examples

Worked annotations for the SKILL.md checklist: use for subtle cases or when several error classes stack. Verify anything beyond this at EKI (eki.ee) or Sõnaveeb (sonaveeb.ee) — don't guess.

## Number ranges and dashes

- Wrong: `1.-4.kl sõidavad` → Right: `1.–4. kl sõidavad`. Two separate issues: a range goes with mõttekriips (–), and the lühend `kl` stands as its own word after a space.
- Ranges carry no spaces: `lk 10–15`, `1918–1920`. Spaces around the dash turn a range into a sentence dash — a different mark.
- Sentence/line dash: mõttekriips with spaces (`05.10 – sõidavad 1.–4. kl …`); in schedule headers a colon reads better (`05.10: …`).
- Sidekriips (-) joins parts inside compound words; it never marks a range or a sentence break.

## Dates

- Notes/schedules: `05.10` is fine; with year `05.10.2026`. Formal prose: `5. oktoobril`.

## Case and verb forms

- Illative for motion into a place: `kultuurimajja`, `Tartusse`, `Värskasse`. maja's standard illative is `majja` (`majasse` is not the standard form).
- Supine after motion verbs (`lähen`, `sõidan`): `-ma` — `lähevad ujuma`, `sõidavad etendust vaatama`. The `-da` form belongs after modals (`tahan vaadata`, `võib vaadata`).

## Names and titles

- Capitalise only the first word and proper nouns: „Punamütsike ja seitse pöialpoissi“.
- Verify a suspicious title by exact-phrase search (`"..."`) plus the organiser's own page/listing — real children's productions can read like mash-ups. If verification fails, report that instead of "fixing" the name.

## Punctuation and quotes

- Standard quotes: „ … “. Straight quotes are everywhere casually — mention only in passing, or not at all.
- Commas are structural: `Ma tean, et ta tuleb.` / `Ma ei tulnud, sest olin haige.` If a comma rule is not certain, check eki.ee rather than guessing.
