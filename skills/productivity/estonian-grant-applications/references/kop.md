# KOP — Kohaliku omaalgatuse programm

Nationwide Estonian community-funding programme run by RTK; applications go through the e-toetuse keskkond (etoetus.rtk.ee) and are handled per county by the MARO (maakondlik arendusorganisatsioon). Re-verify the figures below against the current määrus and the county page each round — they are the standard limits, not eternal ones.

## Money

- Meede „Kogukonna areng”: max 2 500 € per project, up to 90 % of eligible costs.
- Meede „Investeeringud ja kogukonnateenuste arendamine”: max 4 000 € per project, up to 90 %. This is the measure for furniture/equipment/venue investment projects (county funded-lists routinely include furnishing projects).
- Omafinantseering ≥ 10 % of eligible costs, must be monetary (rahaline).
- One application per measure per round; two total across the two measures.
- Abikõlblikkuse periood: max 12 months from the applications deadline; the form requires projekti algus/lõpp = that window exactly („NB! muud kuupäeva mitte märkida”). Activity dates may be a narrower sub-period inside it.
- County round money is split between kevadvoor and sügisvoor (share varies by year and county).

## Applicant / project requirements

- Applicant: MTÜ/SA acting in the public interest beyond its own membership; no KOV or state as member/founder; companies < 50 % of members/founders. Acts for a küla / alevik / vald / linn community.
- Investment measure only: applicant must own the investment object or hold a usage right plus a public-use contract for at least 3 years beyond the end of the eligibility period; KOV written confirmation about needed permits/coordination; all costs paid from the applicant's own account; object stays in public use ≥ 3 years after the eligibility end.
- Procurement: any work/service/goods cost above 1 000 € — two comparable price inquiries' results + choice justification written into the taotlus; quotes are not attached.
- Määrus § 11 (4) content/docs checklist: 11) the >1 000 € quote rule above — results must be IN the taotlus text (count combined single-supplier orders: several sub-1 000 € lines bought together can cross the threshold); 12) disclose simultaneous applications for materially related activities from other funding sources (keep the „seotud toetused” answer defensible); 13) the 3-year vara säilitamise / avaliku kasutuse kinnitus (lives in the form's kinnitused); 14) ownership/usage-right docs + avaliku kasutamise lepingu koopia (when not the owner) + KOV kirjalik kinnitus — item 14 is worded literally for construction investments, so for equipment-only projects confirm with the MARO.

## Ineligible costs (walk the budget against this)

- General: interest, fines, accounting, outsourced project management, loans/leasing/guarantees, representation costs, gifts/souvenirs, depreciation, membership fees, everyday admin/office/transport costs.
- Investments measure additionally: training and event organisation costs (incl. infopäevad), transport/installation services bought from natural persons, food and catering.
- (Kogukonna areng measure additionally: construction/renovation, devices > 100 € that could be rented, food above 10 %.)

## Scoring (hindamismetoodika)

Criteria and weights: 1) goal fits the programme goal + impact on community vitality — 30 %; 2) necessity for the community + community involvement — 20 %; 3) justification of activities + sustainability of results — 25 %; 4) budget justification + cost-effectiveness — 25 %. Scores 0–4; weighted average ≥ 2,5 is positive; ranked by the same average and funded while the round's money lasts. Write each text against its criterion: sustainability, admin/maintenance costs, who maintains → 3; two quotes, market anchors, ineligible-cost hygiene → 4; involvement, everyday public use, who sets usage rules → 2; result-worded goal + wider impact → 1. Investment-specific aspects: criterion 2 — was the community involved in setting the object's usage conditions, does the object enter active daily use, is community participation in upkeep shown; criterion 3 — are additional admin/maintenance costs with their funding sources and a named reliable manager described, plus planned post-project community-involving activities.

## e-toetuse form, page by page

1. **Taotleja** — registry data auto-fills; „Teave taotleja kohta, sh veebilehekülg, ühingu liikmed” free text (purpose, target group, member count, recent achievements — a literal „---” there means unfilled, flag it); „Makse saaja pangas” auto-fills with the applicant's name (an org name there is correct, not an error); kontaktisikud; käibemaks — eligible when not refundable (non-VAT-registered orgs keep prices incl. VAT); riigihange dropdown = „Ei kohaldu”.
2. **Üldandmed** — projektinr, name, start/end, taotlusvoor, „seotud toetused” yes/no.
3. **Sisu** — valdkond + väljund „Investeeringud ja kogukonnateenuste arendamine”; description fields: olemasolev olukord ja vajalikkus; eesmärk ja tulemused; lühikokkuvõte avalikkusele (≤ 500 tähemärki, published on struktuurifondid.ee); tulemuste jätkusuutlikkus (haldus- ja hoolduskulud, katteallikad, kindel haldaja); investeeringu puhul objekti edasine avalik kasutus (+ planned community-involving activities after the project); kogukonna kaasatus; kasusaajad + asukoht/maakond/vald/küla + täpsustus.
4. **Näitajad** — two fixed indicators: „rajatud või korrastatud avalikus kasutuses olevate objektide arv” and „toetatud kogukonnateenuste arv”; start value 0; if only one fits the project set the other target to 0; no adding indicators; per-indicator täpsustus fields plus „sihtväärtuste selgitused” (state how the numbers were derived — reviewers check consistency with the eelarve). Each täpsustus box sits directly under its indicator row — verify the pairing by y-coordinates in a block extraction; the two texts get swapped between indicators.
5. **Tegevused** — activity rows (name, start, end — inside the project period); „tegevuse täpsustus” (step-by-step with each step's purpose); lõpparuande tõendusmaterjal; fields for avaliku kasutuse leping / KOV kirjalik kinnitus / muu lisadokument.
6. **Eelarve** — kogumaksumus, abikõlblik summa, toetuse määr %, toetuse summa, omafinantseering; activity rows + „eelarve rea täpsustus” (full itemisation with arvutuskäik; the place for the two-quote results).
7. **Rahastajad** — applicant's omafinantseering + requested toetus + total.
8. **Esitamine** — digiallkirjastatult e-toetuse keskkonnas (määrus § 11 (3)); representation right from the business register (volikiri only when it is missing); deadline = the submission date at 16.30.

## Report evidence (lõpparuanne)

Before/after photos, invoices + bank statements, delivery-acceptance acts, quote comparison, public-use agreement, publicity proof („Projekti on rahastatud kohaliku omaalgatuse programmi vahenditest” + KOP logo), final report form.
