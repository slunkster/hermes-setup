# Estonian furniture / equipment retailers — sourcing map

Grouped by what they actually stock and how they price. Prices below are of the last check — re-verify before quoting.

## Office & seminar furniture (folding-leg tables, conference desks)

| Shop | Strength | Quirk |
|---|---|---|
| **AJ Tooted** (ajtooted.ee) | Biggest catalogue of folding conference tables + matching table carts; CLAIRE / KLARA / EMILY / Nicke / LYKKA lines; 7-year warranty, Tallinn stock 3–5 days | Prices **ex-VAT** ("Ilma km-ta"); product pages carry a full spec table; site search `/otsing?q=` works well |
| **Ergostock** (ergostock.ee) | Same white-label seminar tables (KAJA 120x60); B2B pricing | Ex-VAT; free delivery nationwide on stock items |
| **Dione** (dione.ee) | FOLD / FOLD BL / FOLD-L / QUICK / YUMI S folding lines, restaurant + conference | Many variants are **quote-only** (price range shown, no cart); top decors incl. antratsiit |
| **Standard** (standard.ee) | Estonian manufacturer, OMICRON / FALDA / FOLD seminar tables, custom melamine decors + RAL frames, cart for 12 tables | **No prices** — request-for-quote flow ("Lisa päringusse") |
| **MK Furniture** (mkfurniture.ee) | Budget folding conference tables (QUICK ~211 €, Kendo on castors ~355 €), consumer-friendly | Prices incl. VAT, free shipping from 249 €, long delivery on non-stock |
| **BRK / Büroomööbli Keskus** (brk.ee) | **Used** office furniture; folding-leg tables 50–80 € + KM | Phone/visit (Pärnu mnt 160B, Tallinn); stock changes weekly; ask for a specific size |

## Event / banquet / plastic tables

- **Tonro** (tonro.ee), **24MX**, **Zown**, **telgirent24**, **Nordica Design** — HDPE plastic "kokkupandav laud" in 120/150/180/240 cm. Cheap (35–65 €) and weatherproof, but the **top folds in half**; they do not satisfy "ainult jalad käivad kokku".
- **Nordica Design** (nordicadesign.ee) — restaurant-grade folding tables (Fold, EC series), mid-range, frequently discounted.

## Table carts (lauakäru)

- AJ Tooted art. 11889 — 12 tables, platform 1200×600, 160 kg, brakes; art. 11848 — 5–10 tables, platform 673×625, 160 kg.
- Standard sells the matching OMICRON cart (12 tables) as an accessory — quote only.

## Consumer generalists (cheap / DIY route)

- **Bauhof** — melamine tabletops (e.g. ERGO 120x60) and garden folding tables; tabletop + separate folding leg kit is possible but rarely cheaper than a ready-made seminar table.
- **Mööblifurnituur** (mooblifurnituur.ee) — folding leg kits (ø50/700 mm; black, chrome, aluminium) if the user wants to reuse a top.
- **IKEA Eesti** — GUNDE 67x67 folding (top folds), MITTZON 140x70 folding with castors (249 €), TROTTEN 120x70 with fixed A-legs (does **not** fold).

## Price mechanics to remember

- EE VAT **24%** (2026). Ex-VAT × 1.24 = what a private buyer / non-VAT MTÜ pays.
- B2B shops show ex-VAT by default and often require choosing "Ettevõte / Eraisik" before prices resolve.
- Used goods are quoted "+ KM" too.
