# EE firm-name law and activity mapping

Quote from the CURRENT Riigi Teataja redaction when answering; the paragraph numbers below
are the current layout (they were renumbered across amendments — do not cite old layouts).

## Äriseadustik rules

- § 9 Äriühingu ärinimi: an OÜ's name must carry "osaühing"/"OÜ" at the beginning or end;
  one company has exactly one firm name.
- § 11 lg 2: the firm name must be clearly distinguishable (selgesti eristatav) from every
  name entered OR reserved in the Estonian commercial register.
- § 12 lg 1–2, 8: not misleading as to legal form, activity or scope; not contrary to good
  morals; written in Estonian-Latin script.
- § 12 lg 3 (the trademark rule): a sign protected as a trademark IN ESTONIA may not be used
  in the firm name without the owner's notarised or digitally signed consent — EXCEPT when the
  company operates only in activities whose classes the mark does not cover. Applies equally
  when activities change after registration (§ 12 lg 3²). Consent formality: äriregistri seadus
  § 40 lg 1.
- Which marks count: EE national registrations, EUTMs, and international registrations
  designating EE/EU. Foreign national marks (UK, CH, US…) do NOT block an EE firm name — but
  they do matter for use-in-trade risk in their own territories.
- § 13 lg 3 restricts using another person's name only for FIE / täisühing / usaldusühing —
  not for OÜs.
- Registrar/notary checks the name against earlier names AND trademarks at filing; disputes over
  name↔mark conflicts go to civil court (hagimenetlus) — registration does not immunise.
- A name can be reserved in the commercial register for up to 6 months before founding.

## Activity → Nice classes mapping (for the consent gate)

- EMTAK 59111 = kinofilmide ja videote tootmine (motion picture & video production)
  ≈ Nice class 41 (film/TV production, entertainment).
- Adjacent media classes to consider: 9 (recorded media, software), 16 (print),
  35 (advertising/business), 38 (telecom/broadcasting), 42 (tech/design).
- The § 12 lg 3 consent gate turns on the overlap between the company's actual EMTAK
  activities and the mark's protected classes: a live mark raised in unrelated classes is
  reported but does not trigger consent. Check the overlap for every live mark found.

## Answer logic for "can I name my OÜ X?"

1. Distinctness: an identical existing name blocks registration; adding a distinguishing word
   (e.g. "Meedia") usually clears it, but short generic stems raise the risk. The final call is
   the registrar's / court's; suggest reserving the name or the e-äriregistri name check.
2. Consent gate: only when a mark protected in EE overlaps the planned activity classes and the
   name is found to use that mark.
3. Residual risks to state: foreign/EU same-field brands (objections to a future own trademark
   application, disputes when operating abroad), domains (not blocking rights — report separately).
4. Report in Estonian, verdict first, register evidence with numbers/classes/statuses, risk per
   aspect, data-as-of date, not-legal-advice caveat, self-check links.
